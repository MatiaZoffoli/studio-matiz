# Prompt per la generazione del logo - Studio Matiz

Data: 2026-10-08. Da dare a un LLM o a un generatore di immagini. Il logo attuale (`logo/Studio Matiz Luxury Monogram.png`) e' il punto di partenza di stile, non quello definitivo. Il prompt e' in inglese perche' i generatori lo capiscono meglio. Consiglio: i generatori di immagini sbagliano spesso le lettere. Per una resa pulita, far generare il solo simbolo e comporre il testo a parte, oppure chiedere a un LLM di scrivere direttamente un file SVG.

## Brief comune (da incollare sempre per primo)

```
Design a logo for "Studio Matiz", a one-person boutique digital studio based in Cesenatico, Italy, run by Matia Zoffoli. The studio designs and builds websites, digital menus, automations and custom digital tools for small businesses (beach clubs, restaurants, hotels, shops, craftspeople) and then looks after them over time. The positioning is tailor-made work with a real person behind it: attentive, warm, precise, quietly expensive-looking, but not more costly than competitors. The brand must feel like a high-end boutique or a bespoke tailor's atelier, not like a tech company.

Style: refined, elegant, timeless, editorial. High-contrast serif lettering (Didone / Bodoni / Didot family, with fine hairlines and strong thick strokes), generous white space, thin precise lines, a sense of craft and measurement. Reference mood: luxury interior design studios, fashion houses, fine stationery.

Palette: warm near-black ink #241E1A on warm paper #F7F3EC. Optional single accent: champagne gold #A8854F, used sparingly. No other colours.

Technical requirements: flat vector artwork, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no textures, no bevels. Must work in one colour. Must stay legible at 16 px (favicon) and look good large on a business card, letterhead, embossed stamp and website header. Crisp clean curves, balanced optical weight, generous clear space around the mark.

Text, spelled exactly: "Studio Matiz". Optional small descriptor in light spaced capitals: "SOLUZIONI SU MISURA".

Avoid: gears, circuits, pixels, brains, globes, lightbulbs, arrows, generic swooshes, stock icons, cartoon elements, script fonts that look like wedding invitations, anything that says "AI" or "tech startup".

Deliverables: (1) horizontal lockup, (2) stacked lockup, (3) monogram alone, (4) one-colour black version, (5) reversed version (cream on near-black), (6) square favicon version. Output as clean SVG vector (or the highest-resolution transparent PNG if SVG is not possible).
```

## Opzione 1 - Il monogramma calligrafico (evoluzione del logo attuale)

```
Concept: a monogram "M" in high-contrast Didone style where one diagonal stroke is drawn as an ultra-thin hairline flowing like a pen stroke, while the verticals are thick and confident. The M should feel written and engineered at the same time. Below it, the wordmark "Studio Matiz" in a refined high-contrast serif, "Studio" in regular and "Matiz" slightly heavier. Quiet, classical, very fashion-house. A subtle detail: the final foot of the M ends in a fine ink-trap or a tiny entry serif, as if lifted from a fountain pen.
```

## Opzione 2 - Il filo e la misura (sartoria)

```
Concept: tailoring and measurement. The letter "M" built from one continuous fine line, like a thread looping through a needle's eye or a tailor's measuring tape folded into the shape of an M. Add three or four very small tick marks along one stroke, like a measuring ruler, almost invisible at small sizes. The wordmark "Studio Matiz" set in elegant spaced serif capitals or small caps beneath or beside the symbol. Feeling: bespoke, handmade, precise. Monoline symbol (uniform thin stroke) paired with a high-contrast serif wordmark.
```

## Opzione 3 - Il sigillo (timbro, ceralacca, ex libris)

```
Concept: a compact seal / emblem. A rounded square or circle with a thin double outline, containing an interlocked monogram "SM" (S for Studio, M for Matiz) in high-contrast serif letters, with tiny details such as a fine inner rule and small dots. Around or beneath it, "STUDIO MATIZ" in spaced capitals and "CESENATICO" in smaller capitals. It must work as an embossed stamp, a wax seal, a sticker on a gift box and a small avatar. Engraved, heirloom, jeweller feeling. Keep lines thin but strong enough to emboss.
```

## Opzione 4 - Il marchio solo tipografico

```
Concept: a custom wordmark only, no symbol. "Studio Matiz" in a bespoke high-contrast serif with italic "Matiz" and one elegant swash or ligature: for example an extended crossbar on the "t" flowing into the "i", or a refined tail on the "z". Optional tiny gold dot over the "i". Very editorial, like a magazine masthead or a fashion label. Provide a companion compact version: a single bold "M" with the same swash style for avatars and favicons.
```

## Opzione 5 - Archi e luce (Romagna, in modo astratto)

```
Concept: an abstract symbol where the letter "M" is formed by two soft arches (like the arcades of a seaside promenade, or two waves), the negative space between them suggesting a horizon line or an open door. Geometric, balanced, calm, minimal; thin outline version and solid version. Wordmark "Studio Matiz" in a high-contrast serif next to it. Feeling: Mediterranean, sunlit, understated luxury, never touristy or nautical-cliche (no anchors, no boats, no shells).
```

## Come usarli

1. Incollare il brief comune, poi una opzione alla volta. Chiedere 4 varianti per opzione.
2. Chiedere sempre la versione in un solo colore: se funziona in nero su bianco, funziona ovunque.
3. Provare il risultato a 16 px e stampato piccolo prima di sceglierlo.
4. Prima di commissionare o stampare, ricerca marchio completa (vedi `docs/BRAND.md` 4e): la forma del simbolo va controllata come il nome.
