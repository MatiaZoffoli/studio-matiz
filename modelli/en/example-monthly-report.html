What we learned

In the first month the hours were not counted. Now we look together at how much was really needed, to set a fair budget for the rest of the year.

The settling-in month in numbers
6.5 hoursReal hours used
9Requests handled
3 hoursHours a month expected from now on

The settling-in flat fee was 250 euro. The hours used, at 50 euro an hour, would be 325 euro: the flat fee cost less, and now it is clear what is really needed. In season requests grow, so it is worth keeping a little margin.
