01

What we understood

The beach club is well kept, with a kitchen and a wine cellar of a high standard. The digital menu leads to a page that is never updated, and when a change is needed nobody replies. The goal is an online presence in line with the quality of the venue, and a point of contact during the season. Number to measure: the table requests that come from the menu.

02

What will be built

  • A refined digital menu, in the style of the venue: updated independently in a minute, with a QR code and a printable version, in Italian and English.
  • A presentation page, clear and fast, readable on a phone, with opening hours, contacts and directions.
  • English texts written by a native speaker for the menu, the wine list and the page.
  • Google listing complete and aligned with what is online: hours, photos, description.

Not included: professional photography, printing of the QR code, paid advertising, new features (for example table booking) beyond those listed.
