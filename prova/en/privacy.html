This page explains which personal data I collect when you write to me or book a meeting, why I use it, how long I keep it and how you can exercise your rights under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

1. Who processes your data

The data controller is Matia Zoffoli, Cesenatico (FC), Italy, [full address and VAT number to be added when the business starts]. For any privacy request you can write to matiazoffoli@gmail.com.

2. What data I collect and when

  • When you write to me by email or on WhatsApp: your email address, or your phone number and profile name, and the content of the message. I use WhatsApp Business, a Meta service that processes data under its own notice.
  • When you book the first meeting: name, email address, optional phone number and notes, through the Google Calendar booking page, which opens in a separate tab and processes data under its own notice.
  • When you ask for a starting price with the estimator: name, email, optional phone, your answers about your project (type of business, what you need, what your online presence looks like today, size of the job), the starting price shown to you and the confirmation that you have read this notice. I do not record your IP address in the contacts archive.
  • Technical browsing data: IP address and technical information recorded by the hosting provider [to be confirmed] to run and protect the site.

I do not collect special categories of data (such as health data) and I do not ask you to provide them.

3. Why I use it and on what basis

  • To reply to you and organise the meeting. Legal basis: your request and pre-contractual measures (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To send you the price and the information you asked for. Same legal basis: pre-contractual measures at your request (Art. 6(1)(b)). Other communications, for example updates or follow-ups, are not sent without separate, specific consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), which is not requested at present.
  • For legal obligations (accounting and tax), if you become a client. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(c).

I do not sell your data and I do not use it for automated decisions that produce effects on you.
